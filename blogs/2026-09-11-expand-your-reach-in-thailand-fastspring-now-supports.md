Reaching your audience in Southeast Asia just got easier. FastSpring is happy to announce that we now support PromptPay in Thailand. Used by more than 60 million active users, PromptPay gives customers in Thailand a frictionless way to pay in Thai Baht (THB) straight from their mobile banking apps.
